Order Agent

Order Agent is a personal parcel tracker that runs on its owner's own Mac. It is not a public service and has no other users.

It reads the owner's order and shipping emails (read-only Gmail access) and delivery text messages, keeps a list of orders and their delivery status, and sends the owner a notification when a parcel ships, waits at a pickup point or is delivered.

  • Read-only: it never sends, deletes or changes any email.
  • Everything stays on the owner's Mac. Nothing is sold or shared.
